      Welcome to Dark Track

      Aircraft announce who and where they are with a transponder. A radar also sees them, but it can't tell who is who. Dark Track combines the two — and spots a plane that switches its transponder off while the radar still sees it flying. That's a dark plane.

      1. Pick a recording of real air traffic (or Live, if the live feed is running).
      2. Press play. Each arrow is a plane, pointing the way it's flying.
      3. A red plane has gone dark. Click it, then press Follow to keep it in view: you'll see where it was last confirmed, where it is now, and where it's heading.
      Colours and terms
      Cyan
      Identified: its transponder and the radar agree.
      Red
      Dark: transponder silent, but radar still tracking it.
      Amber
      Seen only by radar, never identified.
      Grey
      Transponder quiet for a while, not yet flagged.
      Maneuver
      An alert when a plane moves in a way it shouldn't (a sudden swerve).
      Confidence
      How sure the system is of the position: high with a transponder, low on radar alone.
